Recap support

Recap: Simple AI Flashcards — free, no ads, no account needed.

Need help or found a bug? Email support@recapflashcards.com. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I move my cards to a new phone?

Sign in on both (Settings › Sync my cards, with Google, Apple or email): the new phone gets your decks, cards and progress, and both stay the same from then on. Without an account:

On the old phone: Settings › Advanced › Export backup, and save the file (Files, Drive, email to yourself…). On the new phone: Settings › Advanced › Import backup. Importing merges with what's already there; nothing is deleted.

Can I use Recap on a computer?

Yes: open recapflashcards.com in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Your cards are kept in that browser. To have the same cards as on your phone, sign in the same way on both (Settings › Sync my cards), or move a backup by hand (above). Pictures, photos of notes, .apkg decks and reminders are only in the phone app for now.

"Create with AI" says it's busy or I've reached the limit

The AI is free and shared by everyone, so each phone can make a limited number of AI cards per day. If it's busy, wait a minute and try again. Everything else in Recap works offline.

Recap asks before sending text to Google Gemini

To write cards, "Create with AI" sends your text to Google's Gemini AI, so Recap asks first. You can change your answer in Settings › Advanced › "Send text to Google Gemini".

A card the AI made is wrong or offensive

On the Review cards screen, tap "Report" on that card and choose a reason. The card is removed from the list, and we read every report to make the AI's cards better.

What should a spreadsheet look like for import?

One card per row: the front, the back, then tags if you like (for example hola | hello | spanish, greetings). Save it from Excel or Google Sheets as .csv or .tsv, then use + › Import cards › CSV or spreadsheet file.

The leaderboard

Statistics › Leaderboard shows streaks and cards studied this week, for everyone or for the people you follow. It's optional and needs an account (Settings › Sync my cards). Others see the name and username you choose, your streak and your cards this week, never the cards themselves. With "Show me as Anonymous" on, they see your streak without your name, and friends can't see or follow you. "This week" starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Rules: keep names kind. No rude, hateful or sexual words, and don't use someone else's name or pretend to be Recap. Tap a name to block or report it. We read every report; a name reported many times is hidden on its own until its owner changes it, and we may hide the name of someone who breaks these rules for good (also if they leave and join again). You agree to these rules when you join. If you think we hid your name by mistake, email us to appeal.

How do I delete my data?

Your cards live on your phone: deleting a deck moves it to Recently deleted for 30 days, and uninstalling Recap deletes everything. If you signed in to sync, Settings › Sync my cards › Delete my account deletes your account and the cards synced to it (how to delete your account). Leaderboard › Edit › Leave the leaderboard deletes your name and scores there (deleting your account does too). To have the little our AI server stores deleted (a random install ID, daily AI card counts and any cards you reported), email us.
