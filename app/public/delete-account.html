Delete your Recap account

Recap: Simple AI Flashcards

Recap works without an account. If you signed in to sync your cards between your devices, you can delete that account, and everything synced to it, at any time.

In the app

Open Settings › Sync my cards › Delete my account, and confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you sign in with Apple once more to confirm.

On the website

Open recapflashcards.com/account, sign in the same way you did in the app (Google, Apple or email), then choose Delete my account.

What is deleted

  • Your account, and the list of devices that signed in to it.
  • Everything synced to it: decks, cards, study history and study settings.
  • Your place on the leaderboard, if you joined it: your names, scores, who you follow and block, and your reports.

This happens right away and can't be undone. Your other devices are signed out the next time they try to sync. If you signed in with Apple, Recap also asks Apple to remove Recap from your Apple ID's "Sign in with Apple" list.

What stays

The cards on your phones and in your browsers stay there, so you keep studying as before. To delete them too, uninstall Recap, or clear recapflashcards.com's data in your browser. "Create with AI" keeps its own small records (a random device ID and daily AI card counts), which aren't part of the account: email us to have them deleted.

Can't sign in any more?

Email support@recapflashcards.com and tell us how you signed in (Google, Apple or email). We'll help.
