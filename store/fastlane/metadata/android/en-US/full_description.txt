Recap is a flashcard app built for active recall. You type the answer, Recap checks it, and spaced repetition brings each card back just before you would forget it.

TYPE IT. RECAP CHECKS IT.
• For short answers, type what you remember. Recap marks the letters you got wrong and knows a typo from missing accents ("qua tao" for "quả táo").
• Then it suggests Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Confirm with one tap, or pick another.
• Long answers? Tap the card to see them, as usual.

CARDS IN ANY TWO LANGUAGES
• Create with AI from your notes, a photo of a page, a PDF, or just a topic.
• Choose each side's language: English words with Vietnamese meanings, Japanese terms with English explanations, and more, in 12 languages.
• Check every card before you save it: edit, remove, or report anything that isn't right.

HEAR EVERY CARD
• Cards are read aloud in their own language with your phone's voices, in up to 12 languages. Set a deck to Español and English once, and every card sounds right.
• Turn reading off any time in Settings, or tap the speaker to hear a card again.

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
• Sign in with Google, Apple or Email to keep your decks, cards and progress the same on your phones, your tablet and at recapflashcards.com.
• It's optional: without an account, Recap works fully on your device, even offline.
• Delete your account any time in Settings › Sync my cards.

COMPARE STREAKS WITH FRIENDS
• Join the leaderboard if you like: streaks and cards studied this week, for everyone or for the friends you follow.
• Pick your own name, or show up as Anonymous. Block or report any name.

REMEMBER MORE, STUDY LESS
• FSRS, a modern, research-based memory algorithm, schedules every review for you.
• One Study button shows everything due today. One setting: how much you want to remember.
• A daily streak, day-by-day statistics and an optional reminder keep you going.

READY-MADE DECKS
• English basics for Vietnamese speakers (1,000 everyday words), TOEIC, IELTS and SAT vocabulary, plus the capitals of the world, all written for Recap.

PHONES, TABLETS AND THE WEB
• Recap fits phones and tablets, and runs in your browser at recapflashcards.com.

BRING YOUR OWN CARDS
• Import spreadsheets (.csv and .tsv from Excel or Google Sheets), pasted lists, and .apkg decks.
• Math on cards: fractions, roots and powers with "+ Math", no LaTeX needed.
• Pictures on cards, decks inside decks, and recently deleted decks you can restore for 30 days.
• In English and Vietnamese.

NO ADS, NO SUBSCRIPTIONS
• No ads, no subscriptions, and no account needed. Your cards live on your phone and work offline; they only leave it if you turn on sync.
• Back up everything to a file, or export your cards as CSV, any time.

"Create with AI" asks before it sends anything: the text you choose is processed by Google's Gemini service to write the cards.
